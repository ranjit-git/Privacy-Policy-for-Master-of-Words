# Privacy Policy for Master of Words

**Last Updated:** 27/7/26

Welcome to Master of Words ("MOW," "we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the Master of Words mobile application (the "App"). 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully.

## 1. No Data Collection

Currently, **we do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information or usage data.** 

The App is designed to function entirely offline. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we track your device, location, or usage habits on external servers.

## 2. Local Game Data

Your game progress, settings, and in-game currency (gems) are saved strictly **locally on your device** using standard device storage mechanisms. 
*   We do not have access to this data.
*   We do not back up this data to our servers.
*   If you uninstall the App or clear your device's data, your game progress will be permanently deleted.

## 3. No Third-Party Services (No Ads or Analytics)

At this stage in development, the App **does not** integrate any third-party advertising networks (like Google AdMob), tracking services, or analytics platforms (like Firebase). 

There are no third-party cookies, tracking pixels, or device identifiers being collected by us or any third parties while you play the game.

## 4. In-App Purchases (Simulated)

Currently, the App does not process real-world financial transactions. Any "shop" interfaces or "purchases" shown in the game are strictly simulated for gameplay purposes and do not interact with the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or any payment processors. We do not collect or have access to payment information.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any data, it is inherently safe for children and fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EU).

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update the App in the future to include online features, advertisements, or analytics, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
